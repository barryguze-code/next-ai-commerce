# Version 1.7 — Precision Workspace

Marketplace listings bring product identity, status, stock, sales and cost context together in a compact workspace. Account item-code search and separate CSV fields make records easier to find and compare. Verified variation families remain distinguishable from independent listings; unresolved relationships are presented honestly.

Warehouse quantities show case equivalents and loose units alongside each totals. Receiving preserves its reachable Save Receipt action and exception handling. FBA estimates identify their source dates and keep missing cost information visible.

Shared navigation and Columns controls work on smaller screens. Orders retain live, scoped inventory and price comparison rules.

New relationship collection, fee acquisition and carrier confirmation remain subject to separate controlled activation. This release does not copy historical account data or alter stored inventory quantities.
