# Next AI Commerce v0.5.0 - Operational receiving

## Included

- Invoice-first receiving with vendor selection and safe draft staging.
- Native modern KEHE CSV and legacy text-PDF invoice interpretation.
- CSV, TSV, XLS, XLSX, and generic text-based PDF intake.
- Supplier invoice number/date preservation and duplicate invoice/file prevention.
- Product matching by vendor item code, UPC/EAN/GTIN, then account identity.
- Controlled creation of missing global products, account catalogue items, vendor offers, and invoice-sourced cost history.
- Multiple vendor prices per account product with a compact vendor-price comparison view.
- Case, units-per-case, and loose-each receiving with practical prefilled quantities.
- Multiple expiration-date receipt entries without lot-number complexity.
- Short shipment, damage, soon-expired, expired, and other vendor-credit discrepancies.
- Posting guardrails that require every expected unit to be received or explained.
- Value-based freight, duty/import, and shared-cost allocation with preserved landed unit cost.
- Idempotent sellable-inventory ledger posting and FEFO/FIFO inventory navigation.
- Tenant row-level security and server-side role protection for all receiving mutations.
- Responsive light/dark receiving UI with readable operational controls.
