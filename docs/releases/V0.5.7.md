# Next AI Commerce v0.5.7 - Shelf-life control center

## Simple expiration awareness

- Available Inventory now separates sellable eaches, blocked eaches, and inventory that needs action soon.
- Default account policy: stop selling at 10 days remaining and begin alerts at 30 days.
- Expired positions and positions at or below the sellable threshold are marked **Cannot sell**.
- Positions between the two thresholds are marked **Act soon**; longer-dated inventory is **Healthy**.
- Status is recalculated against today's date whenever the page opens.

## Fast warehouse navigation

- One-click table filters show All, Cannot sell, Act soon, and Healthy inventory.
- Search and shelf-life filters work together without a page reload.
- Earliest expiration remains first, and each row shows a clear countdown such as `5 days left` or `Expires today`.
- Color is used as reinforcement, while every state remains readable as text.

## Account rules and action plans

- Shelf-life thresholds are tenant-specific and persist in PostgreSQL with row-level security.
- Administrators and operators can change the thresholds; viewers remain read-only.
- A dated inventory position can save a Discount, Donate, Hold, Remove, or Other action plan with a team note.
- Planning does not silently change prices or inventory. Confirmed execution will remain a separate auditable workflow.

## Database

- Flyway `V27` adds `inventory_shelf_life_policies` and `inventory_expiration_actions`.
- Both tables enforce tenant isolation and retain action history when a plan is changed or cleared.
