# Next AI Commerce v0.6.0 - Live Order Operations

## Exception-first order queue

- The placeholder Orders page is now a store-scoped operational queue with search, status filters,
  expandable order items, mapping readiness, reservations, and shipment confirmation.
- Needs Mapping and Inventory Shortage are immediately visible. Mapping links open the existing
  marketplace mapping studio for the exact Seller SKU.
- Historical and Amazon-fulfilled orders remain visible without affecting local warehouse stock.

## Safe inventory activation

- Each store receives an `inventory_activated_at` boundary when Flyway V31 runs.
- Existing imported orders are historical. Only orders purchased after activation enter the live FBM
  allocation workflow.
- Bundles expand through their mapping components and reserve the correct quantity of every product.
- FEFO expiration layers are allocated before undated FIFO inventory, with older orders receiving
  priority when stock is limited.

## Shipment ledger

- Confirming shipment atomically converts active reservations into permanent negative inventory
  ledger movements for their exact product and expiration layers.
- Viewers remain read-only; Operator, Administrator, Owner, and Super Admin may confirm shipments.
- Shipment requests and store reconciliation are idempotent and tenant isolated.

## Database and rollback

- Flyway migration: `V31__live_order_fulfillment.sql`.
- Version checkpoint: tag `v0.6.0`.
- Previous receiving checkpoint remains `v0.5.11`.
