# Next AI Commerce v0.5.3 - Guided first receiving

## Improved

- First receiving now presents a visible four-step journey: Vendor, Documents, Receive, and Post inventory.
- The current step, completed prerequisites, upcoming steps, business reason, required information, and next outcome are shown together.
- When no vendor exists, the primary action is "Add first vendor" rather than an unexplained upload action.
- Vendor setup distinguishes required and optional information and explains how each value is used.
- "Save vendor and continue" automatically opens Step 2 document upload after successful creation.
- Empty receiving activity points back to the exact incomplete setup step.
- The document dialog explains what the platform will build and confirms that inventory remains unchanged until posting.
- Responsive and dark-theme states are included for the guided journey.

## Design standard

- `docs/UX_DESIGN_STANDARDS.md` records workflow orientation and intuitive guidance as permanent project requirements.
- Template tests protect the Step 1 and Step 2 guidance and automatic transition.
