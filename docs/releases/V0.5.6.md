# Next AI Commerce v0.5.6 - Available inventory and movement ledger

## Receiving table usability

- Increased the Receive products title to establish a clear table hierarchy.
- Restored search and barcode/item-code scanning inside the receiving table widget, where users expect data-table controls.
- Search filters by product title, brand, UPC/EAN, and vendor item code.

## Inventory navigation

- **Available Inventory** remains the operational balance view, grouped by product and expiration date.
- Added **Inventory Ledger** as a separate, searchable movement-history view.
- Added a compact Available Inventory / Inventory Ledger switch on both screens and separate links under the Inventory sidebar group.
- A posted receiving now shows direct links to both inventory views.

## Audit detail

- Ledger rows show the event time, movement type, product identity, signed whole-unit quantity, expiration date, source, PO/document reference, landed unit cost, notes, and recorded-by user.
- Posted receiving batches are traced back to their purchase order or invoice reference.
- The ledger presentation is ready for future order shipments, customer returns, transfers, and manual adjustments without changing the immutable movement model.

## Persistence rule

- Saving a receiving row records a staged physical receipt but does not change available inventory.
- Selecting **Post inventory** creates idempotent `RECEIPT` ledger entries for sellable and zero-cost over-shipped batches.
- Available Inventory is calculated from the sum of ledger movements, so it always reconciles to the ledger.
