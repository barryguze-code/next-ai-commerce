# Version 0.6.7 - Guided vendor invoice completion

## Inventory detail

- Inventory position history now opens as a full-height drawer from the right.
- The drawer keeps its summary, movement timeline, costs, and full-ledger shortcut inside the viewport.
- Desktop and narrow-screen layouts preserve readable movement rows without horizontal clipping.

## Outer Aisle invoices

- Text-based Outer Aisle Gourmet sales-order PDFs are recognized directly.
- Sales order number, invoice date, vendor item code, description, case quantity, case pack, case price, and total are extracted.
- `SO54160` is interpreted as 60 cases of item `50021` and 20 cases of item `50321`, with 12 eaches per case.
- The $57.68 case price is normalized to $4.8067 per each for inventory valuation; the UI presents both as $57.68/case and $4.81/each.
- If the receiver corrects the case pack before receiving, the per-each cost is recalculated from the preserved invoice case price.

## Progressive catalogue completion

- Operationally usable products can be created from invoices even when non-blocking catalogue fields are missing.
- Products created without UPC/EAN are marked `NEEDS COMPLETION` in the account catalogue while receiving remains available.
- Migration `V34__catalog_completion_status.sql` adds the non-blocking completeness status and missing-field list.
