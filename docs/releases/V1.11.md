# 1.11 — Personal collaboration and account workspaces

Deployed release: **1.11.0**, 2026-11-02. Exact CI artifact and production startup verified; migrations V125–V129 completed. Recovery backup retained. See docs/local-huddle-redesign.md for deployment evidence and remaining slow-loading/build-label limitations.

The collaboration workspace preserves the accepted shared table across Active,
@Me, private notes and Closed. Personal priorities are durable and independent
for each user. Priority and Subject grips change the same personal order.
Additional rows group by creation date, with This week and Last week preceding
Last month. Requested week groups remain visible at zero without an expansion
affordance. Original product images take precedence; general tasks use their
owning account logo. A task participant without source-account access receives
an access-required explanation rather than new source permissions.

Account names and headers follow shared presentation standards. Connections
shows the actual marketplace provider and offers display-name editing without
changing connection identity or credentials. Obsolete terminal setup banners no
longer appear across unrelated workspaces; operational warnings stay in
Connections.
