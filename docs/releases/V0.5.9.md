# Next AI Commerce v0.5.9 - Vendor-smart Marketplace SKUs

## Marketplace SKU art and operations

- The table now uses compact black Seller Central and brown Amazon product marks with accessible tooltips.
- Seller SKU has a dedicated wrapping column; price is narrower; four-week sales, 30-day sales,
  estimated fees, future profit, and catalogue mapping remain readable in one operational row.
- Every server-side heading can be clicked to sort, while deleted listings remain at the end.
- Status language distinguishes Active, Out of stock, Inactive with stock, Amazon suspended/problem,
  and Deleted. Deleted source rows are retained rather than erased.
- Four trailing seven-day unit buckets show the sales direction from oldest week to newest week.

## Vendor item code as identity

- Account and Global Catalogue now place vendor item code ahead of UPC/EAN.
- Catalogue imports require product name, vendor item code, and cost; UPC/EAN is optional confirmation.
- `global_product_vendor_codes` records vendor-code product identity without moving account prices,
  discounts, or inventory into the global catalogue.
- Invoice intake and catalogue uploads reuse the same vendor-code-first product resolver.

## Safe automatic mapping

- Seller SKUs are tokenized and matched only to an exact current vendor item code in the same account.
- `2xEA`, `2XEA`, `EAx2`, and `EAX2` endings set the marketplace pack quantity; no suffix means one each.
- A mapping is created only when exactly one account item matches. Ambiguous listings stay Unmapped.
- Mapping runs after listing synchronization and reconciles existing active Amazon stores shortly after restart.

## Shared table behavior

- Ordinary platform tables now support accessible click-to-sort headings in light and dark themes.
- Interactive receiving rows are intentionally excluded so sorting cannot separate expiration batches
  or move a live form away from its product.
