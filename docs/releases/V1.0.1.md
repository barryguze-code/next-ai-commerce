# Version 1.0.1 — Clearer orders and easier packing

## Orders at a glance

- See four weeks of SKU sales alongside your orders to help plan fulfillment.
- Click an ASIN or item-code button to filter the table; click again to release it. Full codes appear on hover, and active filters are easier to spot and clear.
- Larger, uncropped product pictures keep collaboration and relevant actions together without duplicate menus.

## Packing made simpler

- Choose and save packaging for an ASIN and quantity combination, then print a compact 2 × 1 label with up to two items per page.
- Labels show product brand, packaging, quantities, item details, expiry and location without unnecessary footer links.
- For UPS, Medium and OMBox packaging, open Veeqo directly and automatically copy the order ID, ready to paste.

## Inventory and receiving

- Clearer action menus explain stock warnings and guide you to the appropriate inventory task.
- A smaller product-mapping dialog shows search progress, offers product creation when nothing matches, and prevents incomplete bundle lines.
- Receiving menus and disabled selections are easier to read in dark mode. Open invoices offer a close action; closed invoices no longer offer inappropriate actions.

## Quieter refreshes and release history

- Buy Box prices refresh no more often than every three hours, with gentler request spacing and longer pauses if Amazon asks us to slow down. Order updates continue independently.
- Click the version number to see what changed. This history will keep the five most recent releases available as new versions are deployed.
