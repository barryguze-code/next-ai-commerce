# Next AI Commerce v0.5.10 - Marketplace Mapping Studio

## Cleaner marketplace assortment

- Amazon product links use the current orange Amazon mark; Seller Central keeps its compact black mark.
- Listing status shows only customer-facing meaning, price stays on one line, and FBM replaces the
  longer Merchant fulfilled label.
- Mapping badges open the mapping studio directly from the assortment table.

## Single-product and bundle mapping

- A marketplace SKU can map to one or many account-catalogue products with a whole-unit quantity for
  each component.
- The dialog searches the selected account catalogue, supports **Add another product**, validates
  positive whole quantities, and keeps the current table context after saving.
- Manual mappings take precedence. Clearing one explicitly pauses automatic remapping for that SKU.
- Existing safe vendor-code mappings are reconciled after startup and after listing synchronization.

## Profit direction preserved

- `docs/PROFIT_ARCHITECTURE.md` records the progressive estimate-to-reconciliation model for landed
  COGS, marketplace fees, labels, shipping supplies, refunds, and carrier adjustments.
- Bundle COGS is defined as the sum of its mapped components using the inventory layers fulfilled.
