# Version 0.6.5 — Orders UAT wrap-up

Released: 2026-08-31

- Single-product orders render as one compact row containing product, order ID, Seller Central
  shortcut, Amazon status, quantity, item sales, shipping charge, availability, and mapping state.
- Orders with multiple products retain a lightweight order header and grouped item rows.
- Removes the Historical filter, badge, and reporting explanation from the interface while preserving
  the historical inventory-protection rule in the backend.
- Displays `item_price` and `shipping_price` exactly as imported from Amazon order items.
- Keeps profit, margin, markup, and Buy Shipping visibly pending until their operational engines are
  complete.
