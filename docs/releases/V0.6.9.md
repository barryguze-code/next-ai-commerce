# Version 0.6.9 - Resilient receiving refresh and smart expiration dates

- Receiving currency cards use template-native two-decimal formatting, avoiding stale runtime/template method mismatches after a receipt redirect.
- The newest expiration date entered in the current receiving is prefilled for remaining products.
- Up to five recent expiration dates from the account appear as reusable one-click choices, prioritizing dates from the current receiving.
- Searching or scanning a product makes its expiration field the target for a recent-date selection.
- Existing receipt transactions remain idempotent and are not repeated merely because the post-receipt page failed to render.
