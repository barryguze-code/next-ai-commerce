# v1.1 — Easier fulfilment and email invitations

## Invite your team

Send store invitations from info@nextaicommerce.com. Invitations identify who invited you and which stores you can access. Replies go to info@nextaicommerce.com.

## Pack and complete orders

- Open the packing label and Seller Central order from the packing-slip button. Allow pop-ups for the platform to open both tabs. If Chrome blocks the second tab, the packing page provides guidance and a Seller Central link.
- Mark an order shipped from the packing page to move it to Waiting for pickup in this platform. The packing tab closes when the browser permits, and Orders refreshes on return without losing your place. This does not confirm shipment in Amazon.
- After completing the last matching orders, exhausted search and column filters clear so you can continue in the same order-status tab.

## Scan the Orders table faster

- Copy a full SKU with the familiar copy icon and immediate confirmation.
- Read SKU, ASIN and item-code tooltips immediately on hover, without lingering duplicate tooltips.
- Find missing mapping in the order’s warning menu and use the clearer mapping icon.
- Compare four-week sales using compact, coloured bars.
- Clear Filters stays muted until filters are active.

## Discover what is new

The version button highlights unread release notes. Open the right-hand panel to read this release, choose among the five latest versions, or browse older notes from the history links.
