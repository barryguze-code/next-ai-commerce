# Next AI Commerce v0.5.11 - Live Receiving Inventory

## Inventory appears when it is physically received

- Saving a sellable receipt immediately creates an idempotent inventory-ledger movement and makes
  that expiration batch visible in Available Inventory.
- The receipt initially uses invoice unit cost and is clearly marked **Landed cost pending**.
- Posting the complete receiving allocates freight, duty, and other shared costs and changes the
  receipt cost from provisional to final without duplicating quantity.
- Over-shipped inventory is available immediately at its explicit zero unit cost. Damaged,
  short-shipped, mispicked, expired, and soon-expired quantities never enter sellable inventory.
- Opening an older receiving reconciles already-saved physical receipts, including receipts created
  before this release, so the workflow is safely recoverable after restart.

## Editable invoice lines

- Every line with a saved receipt or discrepancy has an **Edit receipt** action, including lines in a
  previously posted receiving.
- Reopening affects only that invoice line. Its prior inventory is offset with a permanent correction
  ledger entry, discrepancies are cleared, and the line is ready to recount.
- Reposting recalculates landed costs after the corrected quantities are complete. No historical
  inventory movement is silently erased.
