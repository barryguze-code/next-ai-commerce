Calculation & data coverage

Four weeks of confirmed FBM sales × each SKU’s component quantity ÷ 28 = daily item demand. At the low-stock threshold, replenish to lead time + healthy target days, with a minimum shared-stock target sufficient for two units of the largest selling SKU pack. Subtract sellable unreserved stock and round up to full cases. Vendor overrides take precedence over account settings.

Forecasts refresh every six hours. Refresh forecast or a settings change queues a new run within one minute. Current listings show last synced stock; four-week in-stock percentages are report-based estimates with visible coverage, using 04:30–23:30 marketplace time and a maximum 24-hour carry-forward per observation. Buy Box %, sessions and conversions are unavailable. Out-of-stock SKUs retain historical demand so a stockout cannot erase a replenishment need. Incoming POs, future expiry depletion and availability-adjusted demand are not included in this first algorithm.
